Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you use our design tools, such as the text and images you upload to create your cards. This includes:

  • Custom text content
  • Design preferences and style choices
  • Feedback and correspondence

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve our services, specifically to render your custom card designs for download. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Security

We implement professional security measures to protect your data. Your designs are processed locally in your browser whenever possible using client-side technologies.

Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies to understand site usage and improve our design tools. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party libraries (like Tailwind CSS and Font Awesome) to enhance your experience. These services follow their own privacy standards and data handling practices.
